About

Welcome to Arãabaik, where the majestic flavors of the Arab world come alive in every dish. Rooted in centuries-old traditions, our recipes honor the culinary heritage of Arabian royalty, bringing you an authentic taste experience like no other.

At Araabaik, we believe food is more than sustenance — it is an art form and a celebration of culture. Our chefs expertly blend rare spices and premium ingredients to recreate classic dishes that have graced royal feasts and vibrant marketplaces alike.

Whether you crave the delicate sweetness of freshly made kunafa, the rich aroma of traditional shawarma, or refreshing, hand-crafted Arabian drinks, every bite and sip tells a story of passion, craftsmanship, and time-honored excellence.

Join us to explore the royal taste of Arab — where every meal is a journey through history, culture, and the unforgettable flavors that define a legacy.

Our Philosophy

At Araabaik, we are dedicated to preserving the rich culinary traditions of the Arab world while embracing innovation and quality. We source ingredients responsibly and combine authentic flavors with modern techniques to provide a dining experience that is both timeless and fresh.

Visit Our Branches

We proudly serve our royal flavors across three convenient locations, each tailored to your dining preferences:

  • Okhla Branch: Fine Dining — Enjoy a regal ambiance and exquisite service perfect for special occasions.
  • Daryaganj Branch: Pickup & Delivery — Quick, fresh meals available to enjoy at home or on the go.
  • Amar Colony Branch: Pickup & Delivery — Bringing the royal taste to your doorstep with speed and care.

Community & Culture

More than a restaurant, Araabaik is a cultural bridge connecting people through the shared love of food. We host regular events celebrating Arabian festivals, music, and art to enrich your experience beyond the plate.

Sustainability Commitment

We are committed to sustainable practices — from eco-friendly packaging in our delivery branches to minimizing food waste in our kitchens. Our goal is to honor the environment as much as the royal traditions we uphold.
